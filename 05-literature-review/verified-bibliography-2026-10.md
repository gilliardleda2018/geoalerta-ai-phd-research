## Source hierarchy and restrictions

- Peer-reviewed article supports general findings and domain context, **not** claims that GeoAlerta itself achieved them.
- Official reports establish agency-specific observations; specify date, scope, and geographic coverage.
- Private `geoalerta-sul` source code, model cards, tests and manifests support only **repository-reported** implementation/results pending independent replication.
- Applicant testimony supports stated personal role, not independent verification.
- Cite exact source and page/section/commit in the final submission. Never infer universal portability or official warning authorization.
