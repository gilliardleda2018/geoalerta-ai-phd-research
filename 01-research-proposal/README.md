# Research Proposal

Working title: **GeoAlerta AI: Explainable and Uncertainty-Aware Geospatial Artificial Intelligence for Territorial Risk Prediction and Decision Support**.

Provisional question: How can heterogeneous geospatial data, predictive models, explainability methods and uncertainty estimation be combined to support reliable territorial risk decisions?

This is a hypothesis for scoping, not a validated research result or a confirmed match with the prospective supervisor's research agenda.

Next: literature review, precise use case, hypotheses, baselines, datasets, metrics, ethical and reproducibility plan.
