## Research problem

The dissertation investigated how information and communication systems, particularly the electronic ticketing system (SEB), could affect operations, service quality and competitive advantage in public bus transportation in Teresina, Piauí, Brazil. Its stated research question concerns how ICT contributed to competitive advantage among the city's transport operators.

## Methodology

The text classifies the study as exploratory and quantitative. It discusses literature review, technical visits and consultation with SETUT users, managers and operators, and the quantification of collected information through tables and charts. Its specific objectives included studying the 13 bus companies associated with SETUT and comparing passenger-record information before and after electronic ticketing. The dissertation describes a central information system and database managed by SETUT.

## Findings and interpretive limits

The dissertation documents the organizational role of integrated information systems, electronic ticketing, centralized databases and IT infrastructure in transport operations. Its conclusion emphasizes the evolution of information-system departments, telecommunications, data processing and organizational roles. **The reviewed conclusion does not present a reproducible causal estimate of electronic ticketing's effect on ridership, costs or competitiveness.** Historical figures in the dissertation should not be described as measured impacts caused by the system.
