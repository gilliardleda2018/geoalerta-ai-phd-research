# SETUT electronic ticketing — historical context

Evidence classification: public institutional source vs author recollection.

- Teresina Municipal Law 3.148 (3 Dec 2002) established electronic ticketing and named operators represented by SETUT and STRANS as partners. https://www.maisfacilteresina.com.br/estudantil/conteudo.ler.php.9.html
- SETUT's Mais Fácil history dates student ticketing to 2003 and expansion to vale-transporte, gratuities and occasional passengers to 2010. It describes contactless smartcards, onboard validators and onboard credit loading. https://www.maisfacilteresina.com.br/bilhetagem.php
- SETUT's institutional page describes its role in electronic ticketing and fare credit services. https://setut.com.br/sobre
- A contemporaneous report (11 Nov 2010) mentions a dual-technology card project, 50,000 cards produced, and a **target**, not confirmed outcome, of 150,000 cards by May 2011. https://www.agencia.baguete.com.br/noticias/teresina-vt-cartao-de-credito-com-intelcav

**Author's first-person account (not independently verified):** Oracle PL/SQL data analysis and exploration of initial electronic ticketing records; support for network-based collection from buses, synchronization at garages, and ingestion into SETUT's central DBMS.

No claim is made that the public sources confirm the author's employment, Oracle version, particular modules developed, or measured project impacts.
