# Master's dissertation — evidence-based academic summary (draft)

**Author:** Gilliard Cardoso Léda. **Institution:** Universidad San Carlos, Asunción. **Year:** 2011. **Supervisor:** Osvaldo Villalba.

**Original title:** S.I como fonte de vantagem competitiva no transporte público de Teresina – PI.

## Research problem
How information and communication technologies, especially electronic ticketing, could improve public transportation operations and competitive advantage in Teresina.

## Methods
Exploratory research, literature review, consultation with SETUT users/managers/operators, and quantitative description of transport-system information. The thesis discusses a centralized ticketing database for 13 operators.

## Findings and limitations
The work discusses operational modernization and potential benefits of information systems. It does not provide a reproducible machine-learning experiment or sufficiently isolated before/after causal estimates of ticketing effects. Historical statistics cited in the dissertation must not be misrepresented as effects caused by the system.
