I am preparing the materials you requested, focusing on GeoAlerta Sul, an experimental geospatial artificial intelligence platform developed around flood-risk challenges in Brazil's Vale do Taquari. The project brings together hydrometric observations, meteorological and satellite data, terrain analysis and territorial exposure indicators within a provenance-aware decision-support architecture.

The proposed doctoral investigation examines whether explicit data-quality controls, event-independent predictive evaluation, uncertainty calibration and safety-constrained model governance can improve the reliability of flood-risk decision support, and how such an architecture can be adapted and evaluated in other cities.

Our repository contains preliminary retrospective model evaluations and documented limitations. I am taking particular care to distinguish those reported results from independently reproduced or prospective evidence.
