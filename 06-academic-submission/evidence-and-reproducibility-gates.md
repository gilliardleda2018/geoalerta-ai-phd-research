## Publication rule
Do not describe any gate as passed without artifact path, source revision, method, execution log, metric and reviewer. Source repository is private; public dossier must contain sanitized descriptions only. A pending gate is not a negative verdict on the system; it is an honest boundary on scientific claims.

## Academic positioning
The present research contribution is a proposed reproducible method for provenance-aware flood intelligence, uncertainty-calibrated prediction and transfer across cities, using the Vale do Taquari as case study. No independent cross-city generalization or official warning authority is claimed.
